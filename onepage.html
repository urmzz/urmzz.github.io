Who are they?

Sleep Token is a British rock band from London, formed in 2016. The anonymous group consists of masked members led by a frontman who performs under the pseudonym Vessel. The band's material is classified into various genres, including alternative metal, post-rock/post-metal, progressive metal and indie rock/indie pop.

Sleep Token debuted in September 2016, releasing their first single, "Thread the Needle". Following the track, the band released their debut EP One in December of the same year, which contained two more songs and alternate keyboard arrangements of all three tracks. In May 2017, the band signed under the independent label Basick Records and released their second EP Two in July. Before the release of the EP, two singles were released — "Calcutta" in May and "Nazareth" in June. In a post dedicated to the exclusive premiere of "Calcutta," Metal Hammer contributor Luke Morton described the song as "a strange and unique blend of progressive metal and broad indie soundscapes." Reviewing Two for Distorted Sound, Matt Corcoran also noted the combination of elements from different genres, noting that "over the course of three mesmerizing tracks, the band fulfills its promise of mixing genres, moving between a light indie atmosphere and a dark Meshuggah-like heaviness, covering most of the spectrum between these two extremes."
